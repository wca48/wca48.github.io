Privacy Policy

Purpose

Webexcep is committed to preserving the confidentiality and integrity of all client information. This policy details our practices for data collection, usage, storage, and protection.

Information Collection

  • We collect business contact details, project requirements, and related data solely to facilitate and manage client engagements.
  • Information is gathered via contact forms, emails, and direct business communication.

Usage of Information

  • Data is used strictly for project planning, execution, client communication, and support.
  • Webexcep does not sell, exchange, or disclose personal information to outside parties, except where required for service delivery or by law.

Data Protection

  • We implement robust security measures to safeguard your information.
  • Data access is strictly limited to authorized personnel handling project delivery and support.

Website Cookies

Our website may utilize cookies for optimal user experience and statistical analytics.

Client Rights

Clients may request their personal data to be updated or deleted by contacting our support team.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically, with revisions available on our website.
